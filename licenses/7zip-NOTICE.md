# 7-Zip

FluentYTDL's updater includes the unmodified x64 standalone 7za.exe from
7-Zip Extra 26.03, Copyright (C) 1999–2026 Igor Pavlov.

7-Zip is licensed under GNU LGPL, with portions under BSD licenses.
The complete upstream notices and license terms are in [7zip.txt](7zip.txt).

Official project and source code: https://www.7-zip.org/

Pinned release and source archives: https://github.com/ip7z/7zip/releases/tag/26.03

Build-time download locations and SHA256 checksums are recorded in
`scripts/prepare_7zip.py`. The bootstrap 7zr.exe is a build tool and is not
distributed inside FluentYTDL.
